# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026 · Applies to Quit Deck: Card Game for iPhone

## 1. Overview

Quit Deck: Card Game is a workplace survival card game for iPhone. We take your privacy seriously: **the app works entirely offline and never sends your gameplay, collection progress, or settings to any server.** All processing happens on your device.

## 2. Data We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information and do not upload any user data.**

- Quit Deck makes no network requests and uses no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
- The app stores only non-personal gameplay data locally on your device using iOS `UserDefaults`: the set of resignation reason cards you have collected, and whether the intro briefing has been seen.
- When you share a card, the card is rendered to an image on your device and handed to the system share sheet. We never access the microphone, camera, contacts, or location, and we do not record, store, or upload any media.
- No account is required, and no identifier is associated with you.

## 3. How Data Is Used and Shared

- Your collected card progress and intro-seen flag are used only to present the Codex and skip the intro on later launches, all on your device.
- We never share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party.
- Quit Deck is a one-time purchase with no in-app purchases; there is no payment processing inside the app, and we store no payment credentials.

## 4. Data Storage and Deletion

- All data lives in iOS local storage (`UserDefaults`) on your device.
- You can clear your collection progress and intro-seen flag at any time by deleting and reinstalling the app. The action takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.
- Uninstalling the app removes all related data from your device.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The app is rated 4+ and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any user of any age. The content is fictional, light-hearted workplace humor; parental guidance is recommended.

## 6. Your Rights

You have full control over your data: it always stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time by reinstalling the app. We retain no copy of your data.
