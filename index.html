Frequently Asked Questions

Does Quit Deck need an internet connection?

No. Quit Deck runs fully offline. There are no network requests, no account, no cloud sync, and no Game Center. Everything — gameplay, your Codex progress, and settings — stays on your device.

How do I play Quit Deck?

You are the last manager standing at QuitDeck Industries. Each day, three resignation cards land on your desk. Tap a card to read the reason and choose: let the person walk, or promise a raise you'll pay for later. Every choice shifts your two resources — funds and morale. Survive all 14 days to win.

What are funds and morale?

They are the two fragile resources you balance. Let too many people leave and morale collapses into a walkout. Run out of money and the company goes bankrupt. Keep both above zero through all 14 days to reach a Survive ending.

What are the possible endings?

There are three distinct endings: Survive (make it through all 14 days), Bankrupt (funds hit zero), or Walkout (morale collapses). Each run is a fresh attempt, and your Codex carries the reasons you've collected.

What is the Codex?

The Codex is a running archive of 40 fictional resignation reasons, sorted into four categories — Absurd, Real Talk, Layoff, and Plot Twist. As you encounter reasons during play, they are collected. Tap any collected card for its detail and a share sheet.

Where is my progress saved, and can I reset it?

Your collection progress (which reason cards you've seen) and whether you've seen the intro briefing are stored only on your device using iOS local storage (UserDefaults). To start completely fresh, delete and reinstall the app — that clears your Codex progress and the intro-seen flag.

Does Quit Deck request any permissions like microphone, camera, or location?

No. The app requests no device permissions at all and shows no system permission prompts. It never accesses the microphone, camera, contacts, or location. Cards are rendered to images on-device for sharing and are never uploaded.

Is an account or sign-in required?

No. There is no account, no sign-up, and no login. Every feature is available immediately after install.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads and no in-app purchases. Quit Deck is a one-time purchase; everything is included and no further payment is ever required.

How do I share a card or replay the briefing?

On a collected Codex card, use the share action to render the card to an image and send it through the system share sheet. In About, tap "Replay the briefing" to watch the one-time intro again at any time.

What are the system requirements?

Quit Deck requires iOS 17.0 or later and runs on iPhone (both portrait and landscape). It is not available for iPad.
